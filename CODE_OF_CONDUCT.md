# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or level of technical familiarity.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Accept correction gracefully and help make decisions auditable.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, doxxing, or unwanted sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as
confidential as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or ban contributors whose behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Follow the private-contact
guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

Good-faith reports and respectful disagreement will not be penalized. Deliberately false or
retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.
